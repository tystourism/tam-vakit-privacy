Tam Vakit — Privacy Policy Last updated: November 20, 2026 1. App and scope Tam Vakit is a free prayer-time and worship companion app. No account registration is required. This version contains no integrated advertising, user-tracking or analytics SDK. 2. Location and online services With your permission, approximate or precise device location is used for prayer times, Qibla direction and nearby mosques. A manually selected city and its coordinates may be used for the same purposes. The app does not continuously track location in the background. Location is not processed exclusively on your device: prayer-time and Ramadan timetable requests send coordinates to AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com); mosque searches send the search center and radius to Overpass servers (ovp.dresel.systems, overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee). City/address search text or reverse-geocoding coordinates may be sent to Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Device geocoding services may also be used. Displaying a map sends map-tile locations to OpenStreetMap or, depending on the selected layer, OpenTopoMap or Esri/ArcGIS. These requests can reveal the area you are viewing. Qibla direction is calculated using device sensors and coordinates. These services can see your IP address and basic connection information when handling requests. Their own logging and retention policies apply; we do not promise that they never retain data. Network requests use HTTPS. Tam Vakit does not create a user account or a centralized location history on its own server. 3. Information on your device Language, theme, selected city and coordinates, favorite cities, notification preferences, prayer-time and mosque caches, dhikr counters and prayer/missed-prayer records may be stored on your device. Times and city information needed by home-screen widgets are shared within the device's app/widget storage. Zakat amounts are calculated on your device and are not sent to a server by the app. Worship records and dhikr counters are not sent to online services. 4. Notifications and external apps Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app does not use its own remote notification server. The optional Android silencing feature separately requests Do Not Disturb access. If you choose directions or sharing, the selected destination, coordinates or shared text may be passed to Apple Maps, Google Maps, WhatsApp or another app you select. Those apps' privacy policies apply. 5. Your choices and deletion You can change location and notification permissions in device settings and select a city manually instead of using device location. You can clear app data in device settings or uninstall the app. Operating-system backup/restore settings may retain or restore some local data; manage these through your Apple/Google account and device settings. Requests about third-party service logs should be directed to the relevant provider. 6. Children and support The app does not request age or child-specific profile information. Location and network requests operate as described above. If you email us, your address and message are processed only to respond to your support request. Privacy, local data and support: tamvakit.app@gmail.com

Lokasi dan layanan daring Dengan izin Anda, lokasi perangkat yang perkiraan atau presisi digunakan untuk waktu salat, arah kiblat, dan masjid terdekat. Kota yang dipilih secara manual beserta koordinatnya juga dapat digunakan untuk tujuan yang sama. Aplikasi tidak melacak lokasi secara terus-menerus di latar belakang. Lokasi tidak hanya diproses pada perangkat: permintaan waktu salat dan jadwal Ramadan mengirim koordinat ke AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com); pencarian masjid mengirim pusat dan radius pencarian ke server Overpass (ovp.dresel.systems, overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee). Teks pencarian kota/alamat atau koordinat pencarian alamat terbalik dapat dikirim ke Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Layanan geokode perangkat juga dapat digunakan. Saat peta ditampilkan, lokasi ubin peta dikirim ke OpenStreetMap atau, sesuai lapisan yang dipilih, ke OpenTopoMap atau Esri/ArcGIS. Permintaan ini dapat menunjukkan wilayah yang Anda lihat. Arah kiblat dihitung menggunakan sensor perangkat dan koordinat. Layanan tersebut dapat melihat alamat IP dan informasi koneksi dasar ketika memproses permintaan. Kebijakan pencatatan dan penyimpanan masing-masing penyedia berlaku; kami tidak menjanjikan bahwa mereka tidak pernah menyimpan data. Permintaan jaringan menggunakan HTTPS. Tam Vakit tidak membuat akun pengguna atau riwayat lokasi terpusat di server sendiri. 3. Informasi pada perangkat Bahasa, tema, kota dan koordinat yang dipilih, kota favorit, preferensi notifikasi, cache waktu salat dan masjid, penghitung zikir, serta catatan salat/qada dapat disimpan pada perangkat.
